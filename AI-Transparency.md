I still reviewed an integrated this code into the project myself.


## My Stance on AI

While I use AI as a programming tool, I strongly oppose using AI to copy, imitate, or take credit for other people's projects, artwork, or creative work.

I am personally against the generation of AI-created images and artwork, especially when it replaces, imitates, or undermines the work of human artists.

Whenever possible, I prefer to support human artists and creators and respect the time, skill, and creativity behind their work.

AI should be a tool - not an excuse to take someone else's work.
